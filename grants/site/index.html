Request a letter of support

Senator Gillibrand does not decide which organizations receive grants, but in some cases she can write a letter of support for an application. Send a description of your organization, a summary of the application, what the money will be used for, and a draft letter to grants@gillibrand.senate.gov.

Need help with a federal agency?

Our office may be able to help with problems you are having with a federal agency. Visit gillibrand.senate.gov or call the Washington, D.C. office at (202) 224-4451.
