A quick guide to your new Fifth Third Bank card
Here are a few things to know about your new Direct Express ® card from Fifth Third Bank.
 Step 1 
Activate your card
 Once your new Fifth Third Bank card arrives, download the new Direct Express ® mobile app* for iOS or Android , use our secure website , or call the number on the back of your card to get started. Set a 4-digit PIN to complete setup.
 Step 2 
Use your card
Your federal benefits will be automatically loaded to your account on each payment day.
Use your card to make purchases and get cash back at retailer and bank locations anywhere Debit Mastercard ® is accepted.
 Step 3 
Manage your card
 Our simple and secure Direct Express ® app and website make it easy to manage your benefits at any time, from anywhere. Track your balance, make transactions, get help managing your money, and more.

When in doubt, contact us
Direct Express ® will never ask for your card number, password, PIN, or security code. Our partners—Fifth Third Bank, Comerica Bank, and Mastercard ® —won't ask for this info either.
Shield the keypad when entering your 4-digit PIN at the ATM. Don't write your PIN on your card. Always take your receipt with you at the end of your transaction.
Monitor your transactions and statements. See a suspicious transaction? Contact us using the number on the back of your debit card.
Scammers often pretend to be financial institutions. Beware of emails and text messages asking you to "verify" or "re-submit" information by clicking on a link. Contact us directly instead.

Get answers to common questions
 I am an existing Direct Express ® cardholder. What is changing and how will those changes affect me? 
 Direct Express ® is pleased to announce our new partnership with Fifth Third Bank. Fifth Third Bank will now replace Comerica Bank as the financial agent for the Direct Express ® card. If you already have a card from Comerica, you can keep using it until you receive your new Fifth Third card. Learn more about the transition 
 How do I dispute a transaction I don't recognize? 
To dispute a transaction, call the toll-free customer service number on the back of your card as soon as possible. Keep all receipts and documentation.
 What should I do if my card or PIN is lost or stolen? 
 If you think your card or PIN has been lost or stolen, call us immediately. We'll deactivate your missing card and send you a replacement. Visit our Lost or Stolen Card page for more information.
